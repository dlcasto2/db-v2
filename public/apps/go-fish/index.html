Go Fish: Discard Pile Edition

Collect four of a kind. Every turn ends with a discard, and the discard pile is a second place to fish.

Rules

Each player starts with 7 cards. On your turn, pick a card and ask the other player for that rank. If they have any, they hand them all over and you go again.

If they don't, you go fishing: draw the top card of the stock, or take the top card of the discard pile (you can see it). If you land the rank you asked for, you go again.

Otherwise your turn ends with a discard: put any card from your hand face up on the discard pile. Once the stock runs out, discards stop and only the pile is left to fish from.

Four of a kind becomes a book. If your hand empties, you draw 5 new cards. The game ends when all 13 books are made or the stock is gone and someone has no cards. Most books wins.
